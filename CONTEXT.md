# mr4x2 blog

The author's personal site: their own travel and tech posts, plus a public log of technical articles by other people that they've read.

## Posts

**Post**:
An article the author wrote, published on the site and listed in "All Posts".
_Avoid_: Blog, entry, page

**Post tag**:
A topic label on a Post. Post tags appear in the Tags page and the home sidebar.
_Avoid_: Tag (on its own), category

## Reading

**Reading page**:
The page at `/reading` that lists every Bookmark. It is not a Post and never appears in "All Posts".
_Avoid_: Bookmarks page, reading list

**Reading collection**:
The single Raindrop collection whose contents are the Bookmarks. Nothing outside it is ever shown on the site.
_Avoid_: Raindrop (as a name for the collection), inbox

**Bookmark**:
An article by someone else that the author has finished reading and put in the Reading collection. Being in the collection means it has been read. Unread articles belong somewhere else.
_Avoid_: Link, entry, read-later item

**Bookmark tag**:
A topic label on a Bookmark, set in Raindrop. It is separate from Post tags and never appears in the Tags page.
_Avoid_: Tag (on its own)

**Note**:
The author's own words about a Bookmark, written in Raindrop and shown publicly under it. It is optional.
_Avoid_: Comment, description, summary

**Excerpt**:
The short summary that Raindrop takes from the article itself. It is used for search, but never shown.
_Avoid_: Note, description

**Read date**:
The day a Bookmark was added to the Reading collection, in Vietnam time. That is when the author finished reading it.
_Avoid_: Created date, saved date
